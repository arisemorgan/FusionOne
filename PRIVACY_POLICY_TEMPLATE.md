## Data we process

**URL Security Scanner**
- URLs you paste or share into the app are sent to Google Safe Browsing and URLhaus
  (abuse.ch) solely to check the URL's reputation. We also open a direct connection to
  the URL's own domain to inspect its TLS certificate and to a WHOIS server to check
  domain registration info.
- Scanned URLs are stored on your device, encrypted at rest, so you can view your scan
  history. They are not uploaded to any server we operate — we don't operate a server.
- You can delete your scan history at any time from the History tab.

**Photo Analysis**
- Photos you select are analyzed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- GPS coordinates embedded in a photo are reverse-geocoded using Android's on-device
  Geocoder, which may use Google's geocoding service depending on your device.

**Football**
- No personal data is sent. Fixture, score, and standings requests go to
  football-data.org.

## Data we do NOT collect
We do not collect your name, contacts, precise device location (outside of the GPS data
already embedded in a photo you chose to analyze), or any advertising identifiers.

## Third-party services
- Google Safe Browsing — https://safebrowsing.google.com
- URLhaus / abuse.ch — https://urlhaus.abuse.ch
- football-data.org — https://www.football-data.org

## Your choices
- Clear scan history anytime (Settings → History → Clear).
- Deny photo/media permission at any time; Photo Analysis simply won't function without it.
